# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, experience or
  identity.
- Criticize work, not people. Assume good faith, and say what you mean
  plainly.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, publishing
  others' private information, or other conduct that would be unwelcome in
  a professional setting.

It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions,
and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Dustin York, at
dyork@typeset.us. Reports are handled privately and promptly. The
maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block people, following
the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban.
